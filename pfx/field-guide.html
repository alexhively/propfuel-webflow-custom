The Talk Track

Open the deck (Google Slides) →

  1. Open — 10 seconds. “Hey, do you have 10 seconds to answer one quick question for us?” Motion to the TV. Don’t pitch — just the question.
  2. Capture as they answer. While they tap, scan their badge and note the one word they picked. In the moment, not from memory.
  3. Get curious. “What association are you with? What’s your role?” Now it’s a conversation, not a survey.
  4. Show value. Their answer pulls a matched case study on the TV — “here’s how an org like yours handled exactly that.”
  5. Pivot to Membership AI. “That’s what we just launched Membership AI for — grab 15 minutes with us onsite and we’ll show you on your world.”
  6. Book + confirm. Set the onsite meeting or log the demo request. Send them to the money booth on their way out.
Day 2 variant. Monday’s TV question flips to “What advice do you have for solving it?” — good for repeat traffic + the post-event report.
