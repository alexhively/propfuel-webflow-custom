# Referral Program — Client Communication

## 1. Announcement email (main send)

**Subject line options**
- A thank-you, if you'll introduce us
- You've been referring us for free. Let's fix that.
- Know an association that should know PropFuel?

---

Hi {{ first name }},

A lot of you have been recommending PropFuel to other associations without us ever asking — and without us ever properly thanking you. We're fixing that.

Starting today, if you introduce us to an association that should know about PropFuel, you pick the thank-you:

- 🍕 **Lunch for your team** — up to $250, however you want it. Sushi in the office, DoorDash for the remote crew.
- 💳 **A $250 gift card**, just for you.
- 💛 **A $250 donation to your association**, in your name.
- 🏷️ **$250 off PropFuel.** $250 back to your association — a discount on what you already spend with us.
- 🎁 **Something else.** Tell us what you'd genuinely want and we'll see what we can do.

**Making the referral takes about a minute.** Go to **propfuel.com/referrals**, add your name and email, pick your reward, then choose whichever feels most natural:

**1. Send them a link.** The easiest one. Copy the link on the page and text it, Slack it, or email it. It opens a short form and we take it from there. Fill in your name first and the link remembers you automatically.

**2. Let us write the intro for you.** We'll draft the whole email — you open it in Gmail, Outlook, or your mail app, give it a read, and hit send.

**3. Let us make the first move.** Drop their work email and we'll send them a friendly note letting them know you sent us, with a link to grab time.

**One thing that matters:** however you do it, make sure your name ends up in the "Who referred you?" box. That's how the reward finds you. (The link handles it for you.)

**When the reward arrives:** after your referral actually takes the demo and it turns into a real opportunity — not just when a meeting gets booked. There's no cap on this. Refer five associations, earn five rewards.

Questions, or want to run a name by us first? Just reply, or email **referrals@propfuel.com**.

Thanks for the ones you've already sent our way.

{{ sender }}

## Notes for the team

- **Segmenting the main send:** the May survey captured each respondent's preferred incentive. Leading with the reward that person already asked for ("you told us a discount would do it — it's now an option") will outperform the generic version.
- **Grandfathering:** several respondents said they've already referred PropFuel with no incentive — Jeff Mendola, Amanda Blosat, Katherine Zavala, Leah Moore, Tom Morrison among them. Worth deciding whether they get retroactive credit before this lands in their inbox.
- **Ease is the whole game.** Jeff Mendola's survey feedback was explicit: the referral process has to be easy, and his prior program only worked when the referral used a link sent *before* the prospect looked at the product. That's exactly why the share link is the promoted path.
