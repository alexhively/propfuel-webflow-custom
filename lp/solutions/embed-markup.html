FAQ

Common questions from associations.

Is PropFuel an AMS?

No. PropFuel is association member management software that works alongside the AMS you already have. Your AMS stays your system of record, and PropFuel adds the member answers it was never built to capture.

How does it handle chapters and national, state, and local structures?

PropFuel segments by the structure stored in your AMS, so you can run and report on chapters, regions, or components separately while keeping one member view.

Can we run different programs for different member types?

Yes. Campaigns target any segment, so new members, lapsed members, and a specific membership tier can each follow their own path.

Does it work for trade associations as well as professional societies?

Yes, both. PropFuel builds campaigns around your own member types and AMS fields. See how it works for trade associations and for professional associations.

Can it support our board reporting?

Yes. Member responses and engagement are captured over time, so you can show the board movement in engagement and retention at year-end instead of estimating it.
