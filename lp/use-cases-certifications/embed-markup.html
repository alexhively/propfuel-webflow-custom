The Problem

Certifications are one of your most valuable non-dues revenue streams. They are also one of the most neglected parts of the member experience.

  1. Interest, then nothing

    A member hears about your membership certification program. They visit the page. Maybe they download a guide. Then nothing. You have no mechanism to nurture that interest into registration, because your marketing tools were not built for individual follow-up.

  2. While they study, silence

    For members who do enroll, the experience is often transactional. They study, they test, they get a credential.

    Nobody checks in during the learning process. Nobody asks how it is going. Nobody connects the certification back to their broader membership experience. Whether it is a membership certificate on the wall or a credential on a resume, it only keeps its value while the member keeps the relationship.

  3. At renewal, a notice

    And when the credential comes up for renewal? The same problem as membership renewal: a payment notice with no conversation about whether the credential still matters to their career.

Four-Stage Lifecycle

From Discovery to Certification Renewal

A membership certification is a journey with four moments where a conversation changes the outcome. PropFuel is there for each one, so more of your certification program’s interested members register, finish and renew.

  1. Discovery

    Surface certification interest in onboarding and engagement campaigns. Ask members about their career goals and professional development priorities.

  2. Registration

    Nurture interested members from exploration to enrollment. Address objections (time, cost, relevance) with personalized follow-up based on what they told you.

  3. Learner Engagement

    Check in during the certification process. Ask how the experience is going. Surface resources. Identify members who may be struggling before they drop out.

  4. Credential Renewal

    Start the credential renewal conversation before expiration. Reinforce how the certification has shaped their career and make renewal frictionless.

FAQ

Questions About Certification Campaigns

How do we identify members interested in certification?

PropFuel weaves certification interest questions into your existing campaigns: onboarding, engagement, and renewal check-ins. When a member says they are interested in professional development or credential advancement, that signal triggers the certification nurture track.

Can we engage learners during the certification process?

Yes. PropFuel sends periodic check-ins during the learning and preparation phase, asking how the experience is going, surfacing study resources, and identifying members who may need additional support before they drop out.

How does this connect to certification renewal?

PropFuel starts the credential renewal conversation before the expiration date, just like membership renewal. The conversation reinforces the credential’s career value and makes the renewal frictionless.

How can an association increase membership certification renewals?

Start before the expiration date and make it a conversation, not a notice. PropFuel asks certified members how the credential has helped their career and whether anything stands in the way of renewing. Members who plan to renew get a one-click link, and anyone unsure can be flagged for staff follow-up.

Which certification milestones can trigger campaigns automatically?

Campaigns can start from dates and statuses PropFuel reads from your AMS, such as a credential expiration date, and from a member’s own answer, such as “I’m interested in certification.” PropFuel then runs the matching check-in.

What if a member shows interest but does not register?

That is exactly the use case for PropFuel’s nurture automation. Members who express interest but do not register get a follow-up sequence that addresses common objections (time commitment, cost, relevance), based on what they told you.

Does certification data sync with our AMS?

Yes. Every certification-related interaction writes back to your AMS: interest expressed, registration status, learner engagement, and credential renewal intent. Your team has a complete picture of each member’s certification journey.
