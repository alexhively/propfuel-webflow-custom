FAQ

Questions About Member Event Campaigns

How do we measure whether our member events are working?

Track response rate by phase, satisfaction and NPS trends across before, during and after, and the themes PropFuel surfaces from open-ended answers. Together they show what to repeat, change or drop, which is what an association event strategy needs.

Can we reach registrants who didn’t attend?

Yes, where attendance is recorded in your AMS. Registrants who did not attend can get a follow-up question about what got in the way and what they would want next, and non-members are routed toward membership.

Can we use PropFuel for day-of event communication?

Yes. PropFuel SMS is designed for real-time event engagement: session reminders, satisfaction pulses, and feedback collection. Text messages get read within minutes, making them ideal for conference-day communication.

How does event data connect back to member profiles?

Every event interaction writes back to your AMS. A member who said they loved the keynote, rated the networking a 9/10, and wants more CE content: all of that becomes part of their profile and shapes future outreach.

What about non-member event attendees?

They are some of your warmest acquisition prospects. PropFuel captures their information during the event and nurtures them toward membership afterward, based on what they told you they cared about.

How do we handle NPS branching?

Promoters (9-10) automatically get routed to testimonial or referral requests. Passives (7-8) get a follow-up asking what would make it a 10. Detractors (0-6) trigger a staff alert for personal outreach.

Can we share event feedback with sponsors?

Yes. The Insights Engine can generate reports segmented by session, sponsor, or topic. This data makes your sponsorship packages more valuable because you can show sponsors exactly how attendees engaged with their content.
