FAQ

Questions About Member Engagement Metrics and Data

Which member engagement metrics should we track?

Start with three: response rate, profile completeness, and NPS and satisfaction trend. Together they show who is participating, how much you know about them, and how they feel. Activity like opens and attendance is useful context, but it does not tell you why a member is engaged.

What does member engagement reporting include?

PropFuel reporting covers campaign and engagement analytics such as response rates, NPS and satisfaction over time, and AI-grouped themes and sentiment from open-ended answers. Because responses write back to your AMS, the reports stay current as campaigns run.

Do we need to run separate data capture campaigns?

No. PropFuel weaves data capture questions into your existing engagement campaigns. A renewal check-in can include a profile update question. An onboarding sequence can confirm job title and interests. Data gets collected as a byproduct of the conversation.

How does the write-back work?

When a member responds to a PropFuel check-in, the response writes back to your AMS automatically, where your AMS supports it. The specific field mapping is configured during setup. You control exactly which fields get updated and in which direction.

What about open-ended responses?

PropFuel’s AI automatically categorizes open-ended text responses into themes and sentiment. Your team does not need to read every response individually. The system surfaces patterns and flags outliers.

How do we measure data quality improvement?

The Insights Engine tracks data completeness scores over time, showing which fields are improving, which segments have gaps, and how fast your database is getting better.

Will this work with our AMS?

PropFuel integrates with 60+ external systems with two-way sync. The write-back works with Salesforce, Fonteva, iMIS, Impexium, Nimble, Novi, and many more. Our team handles setup with you.
