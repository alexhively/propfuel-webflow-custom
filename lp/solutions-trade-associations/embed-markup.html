Illustrative example

The problem

The person who pays the dues isn’t the person using the benefits.

In a trade association, the membership belongs to the company.

Gets the invoice

The invoice goes to one person, usually an owner, executive, or finance lead.

Uses the membership

Staff across the business use the training, events, standards updates, and industry data.

Those day-to-day contacts rarely get asked anything, so when they drift away, nobody sees it. The renewal is then decided by someone who may never have used the membership at all.

Contacts by role

Different questions for every role at a member company.

PropFuel treats each contact at a member company as their own conversation. The person who approves the dues gets questions about value and priorities. Everyone else gets questions about the benefits they actually use.

Primary contacts

Ask the person who signs the renewal what the membership is worth to their business, what they want more of, and who else at the company should hear from you.

Secondary contacts

Ask the staff who use the membership what they rely on, what they need next, and how they want to hear from you. Their answers show you whether the membership is used across the company or by one person.

The company-level picture

Every answer is saved to the contact’s record in your AMS. Looking across all the contacts at one company shows whether that member is broadly engaged or depends on a single relationship. That signal matters most for membership retention, because a company where only the person who pays is engaged is a renewal at risk.

Case in point

NACUBO’s members are colleges and universities, so the person who approves the dues changes when staff move on. One yes-or-no email asked each contact, “Are you the current Chief Business Officer at your institution?” It drew 996 answers. 92% confirmed they were, and the follow-up question produced 67 new Chief Business Officer contacts.

Advocacy and standards

Advocacy, standards, and policy input.

Member companies want a say in the policy, standards, and regulation that shape their industry. PropFuel gives every contact a quick way to add theirs, and gives your team a clear read on where members stand.

Policy consultations

When a bill or regulation is moving, ask members one question about its impact on their business and collect input while the issue is still live.

Standards and technical committees

Ask which contacts work on the technical side of the business and route them to the right standards or technical committee.

Advocacy participation

Find the contacts who want to get involved, from signing a letter to joining a visit to lawmakers, and pass their names to your government affairs team.

Trade shows and events

Trade shows and industry events.

Trade shows bring exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees together, and each group needs a different follow-up. PropFuel runs those conversations before and after the show, so member event engagement keeps going after the booths come down.

Exhibitors and sponsors

Ask exhibitors and sponsors how the show performed for them and whether they plan to return, while the event is still fresh.

Attendees

Ask attendees which sessions mattered and what they want next, and send the answers to your education and events teams.

FAQ

Trade association member engagement FAQs.

Common questions from trade association membership teams.

Can we manage multiple contacts per member company?

Yes. PropFuel reads your contacts from your AMS, so every person linked to a member company can get their own check-ins, and each answer is saved to that person's record.

Can primary and secondary contacts get different campaigns?

Yes. Campaigns can be segmented by any field in your AMS. If your records mark each contact's role, primary and secondary contacts can follow separate sequences with different questions.

How do we see engagement at the company level, not only the contact level?

Every answer is saved to the contact's AMS record, which is linked to their company. Reviewing answers across a company's contacts shows whether engagement is broad or rests on one person.

Does PropFuel work with our AMS?

PropFuel connects to your association management system (AMS) through a two-way sync, so contacts come in and answers go back automatically where your AMS supports it. See the full list of PropFuel AMS integrations.
